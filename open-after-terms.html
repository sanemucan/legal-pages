2. Description of Service

Open After lets you choose apps on your own device to hold during schedules you set, and unlocks them for a number of minutes when you complete a habit first — for example a guided breathing pause, a glass of water, a focus timer, or a walk verified with Apple Health. The free tier includes three habits, up to two held apps, and daily stats. Open After Pro (see Section 4) unlocks all habits, unlimited held apps, and custom minutes per habit.
