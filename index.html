How we handle your data

None of these apps collect personal information, show advertising, or use analytics or tracking. Anything an app stores stays on your device and is removed when the app is deleted. Optional in-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple; we never see payment details. Each app's privacy policy sets this out in full.

Children's apps

Bug Hunt and Play Tots are offered in the App Store Kids category. They collect no data at all, contain no advertising, and place every purchase, setting and link out of the app behind a parental gate, in line with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).
